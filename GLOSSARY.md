# Cal AI

Cal AI records meals and their nutrition in a personal food diary.

## Language

**Base serving**:
The reference portion of a food or dish to which its nutrition values apply. A base serving may be described in grams, millilitres, or servings.
_Avoid_: Selected portion, consumed portion

**Base-serving nutrition**:
The calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat for one base serving, before accounting for the amount consumed.
_Avoid_: Meal totals, consumed nutrition

**Consumed mass**:
The amount of a saved dish recorded as eaten, expressed in grams. It is distinct from the mass of one base serving.
_Avoid_: Serving count, portion multiplier, volume
